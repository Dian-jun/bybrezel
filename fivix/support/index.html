Frequently asked questions

How does Fivix work?

Use Fivix on your iPhone to capture visual feedback, then review and organize it in the Mac app. Copy a structured task for your AI coding assistant.

How do I connect my iPhone and Mac?

Open Fivix on both devices and use the pairing controls in the app. Check device permissions and network connectivity if pairing does not complete.

Does Fivix automatically change my code?

The current workflow prepares and copies feedback tasks for your coding assistant. Fivix does not claim to automatically edit your code.

Can I organize feedback by project?

Yes. Use project spaces and feedback statuses to keep issues grouped and easy to revisit.

How do I report a bug?

Contact support with the app version, device details, and a short description of what happened. Avoid including passwords, API keys, or private source code.
