Privacy Policy

Oodd helps you review and organize photos while keeping your photo library on your device and in Apple Photos.

1. Who operates Oodd

Oodd is operated by Dain Jun. For privacy, support, or deletion requests about support correspondence, contact hello@bybrezel.com.

2. Photos and local data

Oodd processes your photo library on your iPhone. Photos stay in Apple Photos. Oodd does not operate a developer photo server and does not upload your photos to by Brezel.

To restore sessions and avoid reviewing the same photos again, Oodd may store photo identifiers, review history, album preferences, app settings, and pending session information locally on your device.

3. Apple Photos permissions

Photo access is used to display and organize the photos you allow. Changes such as deletion, hiding, favoriting, or adding photos to albums are applied through Apple Photos. iCloud photos may be downloaded by Apple depending on your iCloud settings.

You can change photo access at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Purchases

Oodd+ purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Oodd checks purchase entitlements to unlock paid features. Oodd does not receive or store your payment card details.

5. Advertising

The free version of Oodd uses Google AdMob for banner and interstitial ads. Google may process information such as IP address, device identifiers, ad interactions, approximate location derived from IP address, and diagnostic data for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Oodd does not send your photos to AdMob. Oodd+ does not request ads.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

6. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, Oodd shows advertising privacy choices before requesting ads. You can reopen available advertising privacy options in the app settings. iOS tracking permission is separate and can be changed in iOS Settings.

7. Support and feedback

If you contact support, your email address and message are used to respond to your request. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to handle the request and meet legal obligations.

8. Retention and deletion

Local Oodd records remain on your device until you delete the app or clear the app data. Device backups may contain copies depending on your iOS backup settings. Deleting Oodd does not undo changes already applied in Apple Photos and does not cancel purchases.

To request deletion of support correspondence, contact hello@bybrezel.com. Records required for legal compliance may be retained.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing of your personal data. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Children

Oodd is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated when Oodd features or legal requirements change. The updated date will be shown at the top of this page.
