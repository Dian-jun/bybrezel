Sub Camera Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-14

This document describes how Sub Camera handles user data.

1. Operator

  • Service name: Sub Camera
  • Operator: Da In Jeon (By Brezel)
  • Contact email: hello@bybrezel.com
  • Business address: 55, Sagajeong-ro 54-gil, Jungnang-gu, Seoul, Republic of Korea

2. Information We Process

2.1 User content stored in the app

  • Photos captured by the user
  • Photos imported by the user
  • Notes, folder names, and organization data
  • Markup and edited outputs

2.2 Permission-based device data

  • Camera access
  • Photo library access
  • Location access
  • Microphone access

2.3 Location data

If location permission is granted, approximate location coordinates may be saved with photo metadata at the time of capture.

2.4 Feedback and support

If the user chooses to send feedback, the app may open the user's mail app to contact hello@bybrezel.com.

3. Purposes of Processing

  • Providing photo capture and storage features
  • Letting users organize photos with folders and notes
  • Showing photo-related location information where enabled
  • Responding to support requests and feedback
  • Improving app stability and functionality

4. Storage and Retention

Photos, thumbnails, notes, folder information, and certain settings are stored locally in the app's sandbox on the user's device.

Photos and notes are not automatically uploaded to external servers unless the user explicitly chooses to share, export, import, or send them through another app or service.

5. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell user photos, notes, or location data, and we do not share them with third parties for advertising purposes.

  • when the user explicitly uses a share or export feature,
  • when the user sends an email through their mail app, or
  • when the user enables operating-system-level backup or sync services.

6. Location Data

  • If permission is denied, no location is stored.
  • Users can change location permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

7. Deletion

Data stored in the app remains on the user's device until deleted by the user.

  • deleting photos inside the app,
  • using the library reset feature, or
  • deleting the app.

8. User Rights and Contact

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing.

Support and data deletion requests: hello@bybrezel.com

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in law, features, or operational practices. Material updates may be communicated through the app or relevant distribution channels.
